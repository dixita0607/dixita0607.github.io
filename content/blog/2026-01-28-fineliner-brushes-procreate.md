I created a set of fineliner brushes for Procreate that mimic the feel of real-life fineliner (aka uniball) pens.

## The brushes

The set includes 5 brushes, all cloned and modified from Procreate's classic monoline brush:

- **Extra fine** - For details and light sketches
- **Fine** - Versatile, works well for most line work
- **Regular** - A bit more visible, good for main outlines
- **Thick** - For bolder lines
- **Flexible** - Fully customizable; adjustable size to fit your choice of line

All four fixed-size brushes are designed to feel like real fineliners — consistent size and no pressure sensitivity. The variable-size brush is to conveniently choose the stroke width.
